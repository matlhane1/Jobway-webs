POPIA Compliance

Protection of Personal Information Act • Last updated: 3 Sept 2026 • Pretoria, South Africa

YOUR DATA. YOUR RIGHTS.

1. Responsible Party

JobWay.co.za is the Responsible Party under POPIA. Contact our Information Officer: hello@jobway.co.za / 079 060 8952

2. What we collect & why (Section 8 - Purpose)

  • Name, email, phone, CV, location — to match you to jobs and enable 1-Click Apply
  • WhatsApp opt-in — to send job alerts from 079 060 8952 only if you ticked WhatsApp alerts
  • Employer views — only CVs you actively apply with

    • 3. Lawful Basis (Section 11)

    We process on Consent (you ticked agree + WhatsApp), Contract (to deliver job matching you requested), and Legitimate Interest (prevent fraud).

    4. Your Rights (Section 23-24)

    You can: Request what we have, correct it, delete it, object to marketing, withdraw WhatsApp consent anytime by replying STOP to 079 060 8952 or emailing hello@jobway.co.za. We must respond within 21 days.

    5. Storage & Security (Section 19)

    Data hosted on Cloudflare (SA region), encrypted at rest, access limited. CVs never sold. Employers only see after you apply.

    6. Retention

    We keep your profile until you delete account. Applications kept 2 years for labour compliance. You can request deletion anytime.

    If not satisfied, contact Information Regulator: complaints.IR@justice.gov.za • 012 406 4818
