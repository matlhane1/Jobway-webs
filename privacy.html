Privacy Policy

Last updated: 5 September 2026 • JobWay.co.za • Pretoria, South Africa • POPIA Compliant

1. What we collect

We collect email, CV (from storage bucket `cvs`), profile data (from `avatars`), company logos (`company-logos`), job applications, and WhatsApp alerts you opt into via 079 060 8952.

2. POPIA Compliant

We comply with the Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA). Your data is stored securely via Supabase / Cloudflare (SA region where possible). You can request access, correction or deletion: hello@jobway.co.za. We retain active accounts for duration of account + 12 months.

3. How we use your data

  • Match you to jobs, 1-Click Apply, send alerts via 079 060 8952
  • Employers see only CVs you applied with — we do not sell your data
  • Secure file hosting via Supabase Storage with RLS policies
